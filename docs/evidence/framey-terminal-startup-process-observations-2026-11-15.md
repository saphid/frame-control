This test-only observer adds numeric process identity, CPU ticks and child-list coverage immediately before the first RPC and when an RPC fails. It follows same-UID descendants, checks identity again after reading, and scans child lists from non-leader threads. Reads are limited to 4,096 bytes plus one overflow-detection byte per procfs file, 16 processes and 16 threads per process; missing thread details are capped at four per process with the full count retained. Clipping, unavailable records, clock-tick units and observation duration are explicit. Observations are best effort, not an atomic process-tree snapshot or a stack/scheduling diagnosis. Commands, environment, arguments and plugin input are not retained.
