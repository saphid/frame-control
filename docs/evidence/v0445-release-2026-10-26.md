The annotated `v0.4.45` tag dereferences to main merge commit
`212f0af35c86eb651d5d23bfe0b1e6bf50c528b8` from PR #320. The app and
lockfile both report 0.4.45. [Post-merge main checks](https://github.com/saphid/frame-control/actions/runs/40309949732)
passed all seven jobs, including iOS and fake Frame end-to-end tests. The
[tagged release workflow](https://github.com/saphid/frame-control/actions/runs/40310897979)
passed its draft, Linux, Windows, Apple Silicon Mac, Intel Mac and checksum

that does not verify a physical display, FUSE mount or headset connection.

code. Synthetic OpenVR input, setting persistence and scroll-button

## Publication and limits

`scripts/publish-release.sh v0.4.45` published
[v0.4.45](https://github.com/saphid/frame-control/releases/tag/v0.4.45) at
2026-10-26 11:02:08 UTC. GitHub's latest-release API returned
`draft=false`, `prerelease=false`, the expected tag, ten installers,
`SHA256SUMS` and `update.json`. A fresh public latest-download of
`update.json` reported version 0.4.45, the stable release page, ten hashed
assets and the worn-VR limits in its notes.

The official exact-version Decky catalog remains **4 works / 49 partial /
52 broken / 11 N/A** out of 116. AlarMe's tested silent timer, preset and
stopwatch workflows do not establish its audio, reminders, sleep controls,
closed-panel notifications or VR panel. The real Frame exercise used the
repository sample, not an official plugin. No wearer confirmation was
available. Physical controller use, optical readability, official-plugin
VR rendering, audible output and the VR keyboard remain unverified. The
hourly shared-lock worn-check schedule stays enabled.
